On-call runbook — customer feedback app
=======================================

Working notes for the support-engineering rotation. Update as you learn.


"The team says no alert arrived for an escalation"
--------------------------------------------------
Work the chain in order; stop at the first mismatch:

1. Open the feedback record in the dashboard. Is there an escalation at
   all? If not, this is an escalation-policy question, not a notification
   question — check the item's category and urgency against the policy.
2. If there is an escalation, look at its notification rows on the detail
   page. A row with status "suppressed" carries an explanation in its
   detail field — that is usually the whole answer.
3. If there are NO notification rows at all for the escalation, the master
   notifications switch was almost certainly off when the escalation was
   created. That leaves no database trace by design — check the
   application log around the escalation's timestamp, and check what the
   notification configuration was at the time.
4. Confirm the recipient team id in the active configuration matches the
   inbox the team actually watches.

History worth knowing: during the June alert-volume review the immediate-
alert urgency bar was temporarily raised (see CHANGELOG 1.3.x), and the
alert channel is switched off entirely during maintenance windows. Both
have produced "where was my alert?" tickets after the fact.


Maintenance windows
-------------------
Procedure: set NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED=false and restart; intake keeps
running and customers are unaffected. Escalations created during the
window do NOT page anyone and leave no outbox row — sweep the dashboard
for unresolved escalations at the end of the window before re-enabling.
Forgetting the sweep is the classic failure mode; it has happened.


Log crib sheet
--------------
Logs go to logs/app.log (and stdout). One line per decision:

  feedback received          intake accepted a submission
  priority assessed          triage verdict, includes level= and rule=
  escalation created         includes the reason string
  escalation skipped         high item that did NOT meet escalation policy
  notification dispatched    alert written to outbox as sent
  notification suppressed    two variants — read the message text; one
                             cites the urgency threshold, the other the
                             disabled flag
  feedback resolved          terminal status applied

grep by feedback_id= to reconstruct one item's full story.


Useful queries (sqlite3 instance/feedback.db)
---------------------------------------------
Unresolved critical items:
  SELECT id, customer_name, status FROM feedback
  WHERE urgency='critical' AND status != 'resolved';

Escalations never successfully notified:
  SELECT id, feedback_id, reason FROM escalations
  WHERE notified_at IS NULL;

Suppressed alerts and why:
  SELECT escalation_id, detail FROM notifications
  WHERE status='suppressed';
